# Hacktoberfest 2026 Outreach Plan

This document tracks how KubeStellar Console will prepare for, promote, and operate a contributor-friendly Hacktoberfest 2026 campaign.

## Goals

- Welcome at least **5 new contributors**
- Keep first-time contributors unblocked with fast maintainer responses
- Turn docs, test, translation, and small product issues into clear onboarding paths

## Timeline

### July 2026 — prep

- [ ] Audit open issues for clear reproduction steps and acceptance criteria
- [ ] Create a starter set of `good-first-issue` candidates
- [ ] Add screenshots, links, and "where to change" notes to onboarding issues
- [ ] Refresh contributor docs and community links

### August 2026 — promotion

- [ ] Announce participation in CNCF Slack and KubeStellar channels
- [ ] Publish a short blog/social post linking to starter issues
- [ ] Ask maintainers to subscribe to issue notifications for faster response
- [ ] Prepare a tracking board for first-time contributor PRs

### October 2026 — event operations

- [ ] Reconfirm labels and issue inventory on October 1
- [ ] Acknowledge new contributors within 24 hours
- [ ] Review or request follow-up on active PRs within 2 business days
- [ ] Aim for merge, or a clear next-step comment, within 7 days when CI is green

## Issue tagging strategy

Use both labels when an issue is appropriate for Hacktoberfest:

- `good-first-issue` — clear scope, low risk, strong maintainer support
- `hacktoberfest` — intentionally prepared for October outreach

Issue checklist before adding labels:

- [ ] One-sentence problem statement
- [ ] Exact files or directories likely to change
- [ ] Acceptance criteria in bullets
- [ ] Notes about whether code, docs, tests, or translations are enough
- [ ] A maintainer or reviewer assigned to watch the issue

## Contributor onboarding checklist

For every first-time contributor issue or PR:

- [ ] Respond within **24 hours**
- [ ] Link the contributor to [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [docs/COMMUNITY.md](../COMMUNITY.md)
- [ ] Confirm whether the issue is still available
- [ ] Answer setup questions in-thread so future contributors benefit
- [ ] Keep review cycles short and concrete
- [ ] Target a **7-day merge SLA** for ready PRs with passing CI

## Example maintainer response

> Thanks for picking this up. The issue is available. Start with the acceptance checklist above, and if you get blocked on setup or testing, ask in-thread or in CNCF Slack `#kubestellar-dev`. We try to respond within 24 hours.

## Success metrics

- **5+ new contributors** during the 2026 campaign
- 90% of first responses posted within 24 hours
- Median merge time for ready Hacktoberfest PRs at or under 7 days
- At least 3 merged docs/test/translation PRs from first-time contributors

## Reporting cadence

- Weekly issue inventory review in September and October
- Mid-month summary during the October community call
- End-of-event retrospective with wins, blockers, and follow-up issues
